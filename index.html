PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

Education Is the Foundation of a Successful Future

With 29 years of experience in teaching Mathematics, I have always believed that education is not merely about securing marks or ranks—it is about building knowledge, discipline, confidence, character and a strong foundation for the future.

We established Sree Vaishnavi Junior College, Bhongir, in 2013 with a clear vision: to provide quality education in a disciplined, corporate-level academic environment for the students of Bhongir and the surrounding rural areas.

Our philosophy is simple: we give priority to quality education rather than the number of admissions. To ensure individual attention and better academic monitoring, we restrict each class to around 35 students.

This enables our experienced and dedicated faculty to focus on the overall development of every student.

Madhira Mallesham Principal
